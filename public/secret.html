Make a session secret

Your builder needs one long, random password called SESSION_SECRET. It keeps everyone's HubSpot sign-in private. Nobody has to remember it - you paste it once into your hosting settings.

This secret is made right here in your browser. It is not sent anywhere or saved. Reloading the page makes a different one.

Where to paste it

  • Render: your service → Environment → SESSION_SECRET → paste → Save changes. (Render can also make one for you.)
  • Vercel: your project → Settings → Environment Variables → SESSION_SECRET → paste → Save, then Redeploy.
  • Cloudflare: your worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add → type Secret, name SESSION_SECRET.
  • Docker / your own server: set the environment variable SESSION_SECRET to this value.

Good to know

  • Keep it private, like a password. Do not put it in email or chat.
  • Changing it later signs everyone out of HubSpot in the builder. They just click Connect HubSpot again.
